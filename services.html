Services

Our services are tailored to the individual needs of our clients. While every engagement is unique, the core areas where we provide the most value include:

Business-focused Services

Accounting Support and Bookkeeping
Maintaining or helping you and your staff maintain clear and accurate records; the foundation for all other financial services.
Issuing Financial Statements
Professional reporting to help you secure lending, satisfy stakeholders, or simply track performance.
Strategic Tax Planning and Mitigation
Proactive strategies designed to legally minimize your tax burden and eliminate year-end surprises.
Tax Preparation
Comprehensive filing for S-Corps, LLCs, Partnerships, and C-Corps, ensuring full compliance with the latest tax codes.
IRS Representation
Professional advocacy to resolve audits, notices, or disputes, giving you peace of mind and protecting your interests.
Advisory and Fractional CFO Services
High-level financial advice to help you manage cash flow, track KPIs, and make data-driven decisions.

Individual-focused Services

Issuing Personal Financial Statements
Detailed snapshots of your net worth, often required for high-level financing or estate planning.
Short and Long-term Tax Planning
Multi-year strategies to protect your wealth through life transitions, retirement, and shifting tax laws.
Tax Preparation
Expert tax preparation for complex individual returns, with a focus on compliance, high-net-worth reporting, and the detailed tax treatment of business and investment holdings.
IRS Representation
Expert resolution of personal tax notices or audits so you never have to deal with the IRS alone.
